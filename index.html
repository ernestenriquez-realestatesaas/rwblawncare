    How do I book?
    Tap any Book Now button to text Cody at 812-243-8146, or fill out the quote form below. Your lot size and the services you want get sent right to his phone.
    
      
    Are quotes really free?
    Yes. Cody looks at the job and gives you a price before any work starts.
    
      
    Why do you ask for my lot size?
    It's the biggest factor in how long a job takes, so it lets Cody give you a faster, more accurate quote. If you don't know it, pick "Not sure" and he'll take a look.
    
      
    Which grass should I pick for new sod?
    St. Augustine is the San Antonio standard and handles shade under oaks. Bermuda loves full sun and heavy foot traffic. Zoysia is dense and soft and needs fewer cuts. Cody will recommend the right one for your yard.
    
      
    How fast will I hear back?
    Your text goes straight to Cody's phone, not a call center, so you're talking to the owner from the first message.
    
      
    Do you do one-time jobs?
    Yes. One-time cuts, cleanups, trims and pressure washing are welcome, along with regular weekly or bi-weekly service.
    
      
    What areas do you cover?
    San Antonio and surrounding cities including Stone Oak, Helotes, Converse, Schertz, Cibolo, Boerne and New Braunfels. If you're nearby, just ask.
